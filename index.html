Can you set up at my home, or only at a venue?

Both. We set up the tent, kitchen and decoration at your home, lawn or street, or at any venue you have booked.

Can I hire only the tent house, without catering or decoration?

Yes. Take a single service or hand over the complete event under one contract.

Yes, complete arrangements. Share the date, place and expected crowd so we can plan the ground, stage, sound and crew.
